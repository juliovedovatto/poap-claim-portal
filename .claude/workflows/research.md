# workflow: research

External research (APIs, libraries, pricing, security, behavior).

## Steps
1. **researcher** (Sonnet) — search, evaluate, synthesize a focused brief with sources.
2. **planner** (Opus) — only if the research drives a complex decision; else you synthesize.

## Don't
- Don't do external research with raw read tools when a researcher fits — it gathers + synthesizes better and keeps raw pages out of context.
